🐾 Pawprint Motion Map

  • Even Spacing: Moving at constant speed.
  • Spreading Apart: Speeding up (accelerating).
  • Bunching Close: Slowing down (braking).
  • Vertical Stack: Stationary at rest (v = 0).

📈 Position-Time (x-t) Graphs

  • Slope = Velocity: Steeper line = Faster speed.
  • Positive Slope (/): Moving Right (+).
  • Negative Slope (\): Moving Left (-).
  • Flat Horizontal Line: Stopped at rest (v = 0).
  • Curved Parabola: Changing speed (acceleration).

📉 Velocity-Time (v-t) Graphs

  • Above Zero Line: Moving Right (+).
  • Below Zero Line: Moving Left (-).
  • On Zero Axis: Stopped at rest (v = 0).
  • Flat Horizontal Line: Constant velocity (a = 0).
  • Slanted Line: Accelerating (Slope = Acceleration).
