# 🚗 Graph That Motion — Student Investigation Handout
**The Thinking Experiment | PhysicsKit Kinematics Module**

---

## 🎯 Learning Objectives
- Translate between **physical motion**, **motion maps (ticker-tape dots)**, **Position-Time ($x$-$t$)**, and **Velocity-Time ($v$-$t$)** graphs.
- Interpret the physical meaning of graphical features:
  - **Slope of $x$-$t$ graph** represents **velocity ($v$)**.
  - **Slope of $v$-$t$ graph** represents **acceleration ($a$)**.
  - **Flat horizontal lines** represent constant value (stationary on $x$-$t$, constant speed on $v$-$t$).
  - **Curved lines on $x$-$t$** represent changing speed (acceleration).

---

## 🧭 Motion Map Reference
A **motion map** drops a point at regular time intervals ($\Delta t = 0.30\text{ s}$):
- **Constant Velocity:** Points are evenly spaced.
- **Speeding Up:** Points get progressively farther apart.
- **Slowing Down:** Points get progressively closer together.
- **At Rest (Stationary):** Points cluster/stack vertically at the same location.
